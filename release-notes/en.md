# Release notes

What changes for you in each version of the app. The newest version is first.

## Unreleased

- You sign in with your Google account, or tap **Use offline** to use the app with no account. The administrator approves each new account. Your properties stay on this device.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-10-24

- Add your properties: tap `+`, write a name and, if you want, an address. Tap a property to see, edit or delete it.

## 0.1.1 - 2026-10-24

- First version of Landlord. It works offline, installs on Android and has light and dark themes in English and Spanish. The tools to manage your houses come next.
